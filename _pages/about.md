<div class="prose biography"><p class="lead">I am Professor of Mathematics and Computing Education at Tallinn University, working at the intersection of educational research, teacher education, technology and innovation.</p><p>My professional trajectory began in school mathematics and physics teaching and school leadership. It developed through educational technology and teacher education into research and the design of educational tools and services.</p><h2>Designing the change we study</h2><p>A recurring theme of my work is moving beyond studying educational innovation toward designing, implementing and evaluating it in authentic educational settings. That perspective brings together mathematics and computing education, learning design, digital competence and educational technology.</p><p>I completed a master's degree in Educational and Training Systems Design at the University of Twente in 1997 and a doctorate at Tallinn University in 2013. My doctoral work focused on pedagogy-driven design of virtual learning environments.</p><h2>Research with a practical setting</h2><p>Today, this approach connects classroom research in mathematics, digital competence assessment, professional development and educational design. The portfolio ranges from LAHEMATIX and DigitalMirror to DigiProf and learning-design tools.</p><p>International work provides another setting for these questions: how research-based frameworks can be adapted to different institutions, educational systems and professional communities.</p><h2>A continuing conversation</h2><p>I welcome collaboration with researchers, teacher educators, schools, public organisations and EdTech teams interested in connecting sound educational ideas with usable designs.</p><p><a href="{{ '/cv/' | relative_url }}">Academic CV →</a> · <a href="{{ '/knowledge-services/' | relative_url }}">Knowledge services →</a></p><p class="source-note">Academic details: <a href="https://www.etis.ee/CV/Mart_Laanpere/eng/">ETIS CV</a> and <a href="https://www.tlu.ee/en/node/106574">Tallinn University</a>.
