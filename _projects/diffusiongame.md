## Learning through decisions

DiffusionGame places players in the role of a change agent introducing an innovation into an organisation. The public game describes its foundation in Everett Rogers' Diffusion of Innovations theory.

## Experience and reflection

A facilitator shares a join code, players choose strategies, and the group reflects on their decisions. The platform describes multiplayer sessions, configurable scenarios and research data exports.
