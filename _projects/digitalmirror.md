## Making sense of change

DigitalMirror supports organisations in reflecting on digital maturity. Employees contribute evidence to a shared assessment, which can inform participatory strategy development and an action plan.

## Research into practice

The approach connects pedagogical innovation, organisational change and technology infrastructure. It has been developed and used in Estonia to support educational institutions' digital transformation.
