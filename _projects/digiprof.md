## Professional learning for digital pedagogy

DigiProf connects teacher digital competence with professional development in Moldova. The Clasa Viitorului environment provides access to programmes and course resources.

## My contribution

ETIS records my leadership of DigiProf-A (2024–2025), the B-level teacher training programme (2025), and the C-level programme (2025–2026). These projects are supported by the Estonian Centre for International Development, ESTDEV.

## Design focus

The programme sits at the intersection of teacher learning, digital competence and practical classroom use. Its learning environment makes progression through professional development visible across programme levels.
