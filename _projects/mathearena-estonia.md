## Connecting an app with a curriculum

MatheArena Estonia is a planned localisation and spin-off initiative arising from academia–startup collaboration associated with EdTech Talents. The Estonian company and product launch are planned for late 2026; they are not presented here as completed.

The Austrian source product combines adaptive mathematics practice with game elements. Localisation requires attention to language, curriculum, mathematical representations and classroom use.

## Research and teacher education

ETIS records my supervision of 2026 master's work on the localisation and adaptation of MatheArena to the Estonian curriculum. This connects educational design decisions with teacher education and research.
